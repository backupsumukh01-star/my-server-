Frequently asked.

Still have questions? Our support team is online 24/7.

Do I need to pass a credit check?

No. TrustCard is a prepaid product secured by your connected on-chain balance. No credit check, no impact to your score.

Which wallet do I need?

TrustCard is built exclusively for Trust Wallet. Download Trust Wallet on iOS or Android, then scan the QR code to link your card.

How are rewards paid out?

Rewards settle on-chain to your connected wallet address in the asset of your choice - BTC, ETH, USDT, or USDC - within 24 hours of each transaction.

Is there an annual fee?

No annual fee on any tier. Ultra tier requires a minimum connected balance; Premium and Exclusive have no balance requirement.

How is my wallet kept secure?

TrustCard never takes custody of your assets and never holds your private keys. All operations are signed by your wallet and verified on-chain.
