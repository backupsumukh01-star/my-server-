FAQ

Frequently asked

01Do I need a credit check?

No. TrustCard is a prepaid product secured by your connected on-chain balance. There is no credit check and no impact on your credit score.

02Which wallet do I need?

TrustCard works exclusively with Trust Wallet on iOS or Android. Scan the QR code during application to link your wallet — other wallets are not supported.

03How are rewards paid out?

Rewards settle on-chain to your connected wallet within 24 hours of each purchase. You can choose BTC, ETH, USDT, or USDC.

04Is there an annual fee?

No annual fee on any tier. Ultra requires a minimum connected balance; Premium and Exclusive have no balance requirement.

05How is my wallet kept secure?

TrustCard never takes custody of your assets or holds your private keys. Every action is signed in Trust Wallet and verified on-chain.

06How long does approval take?

Most applications are approved in under 60 seconds after wallet connection. Your virtual card is issued instantly once eligibility is confirmed.

07Where can I use my TrustCard?

Spend at 80 million+ merchants worldwide anywhere Visa and Mastercard are accepted — online, in-store, and abroad.

08What are the card tiers?

Premium, Exclusive, and Ultra unlock based on your connected balance. Higher tiers earn more cashback and include perks like lounge access and concierge support.

09Can I use Apple Pay or Google Pay?

Yes. Add your virtual or physical TrustCard to Apple Pay and Google Pay in seconds and pay contactlessly from your phone or watch.

10When will I receive my physical card?

Your virtual card is live within minutes. Physical cards ship in 3–5 business days with tracked delivery to your verified address.

Trust Wallet Card

A Trust Wallet card closes the gap between the balance you hold on-chain and the purchases you make every day. Instead of off-ramping crypto before each transaction, you connect Trust Wallet once and pay at millions of merchants worldwide. TrustCard is designed for wallet users who want a direct path from assets to checkout—without a separate banking app or a chain of manual transfers.

For many holders, the practical benefit is speed and clarity. Eligibility is linked to your connected balance, rewards settle back to the wallet you already use, and most applications complete in under a minute. Whether you are buying groceries, booking travel, or paying for a subscription, a Trust Wallet card keeps spending inside the ecosystem you already rely on.

Trust Wallet Crypto Card

A crypto card built for Trust Wallet works differently from a traditional credit product. You are not borrowing against a credit line or waiting for a bank to approve your file. Your connected assets provide the backing layer, which means no credit check and no impact on your credit score.

That model suits people who earn, hold, and move digital assets regularly. When you spend, the payment runs through card networks merchants already accept, while cashback can return in BTC, ETH, USDT, or USDC within 24 hours. It is a straightforward way to put wallet balance to work without giving up custody of your keys.

Trust Wallet Visa Card

Visa acceptance is what makes a Trust Wallet Visa card useful beyond the crypto community. Grocery stores, gas stations, airlines, and online retailers already process Visa—so you do not need merchants to accept crypto directly. You pay the way they expect, and your wallet stays at the center of the flow.

TrustCard offers both virtual and physical formats. Add your card to Apple Pay or Google Pay right after approval, or request a premium metal card for in-person use. Higher balance tiers unlock stronger rewards and extras like lounge access, giving active spenders a reason to route everyday purchases through one card.

Trust Wallet Virtual Card

A virtual Trust Wallet card is issued within minutes of approval—useful when you need to pay online today rather than wait for delivery. The virtual number works across e-commerce checkouts, app subscriptions, and digital wallets, so you can split spending between browser payments and phone tap-to-pay without managing two separate products.

Security remains wallet-native throughout. TrustCard never takes custody of your funds or holds your private keys. Authorizations happen through Trust Wallet, and you can review activity as it happens. For online-first users, a virtual card removes friction while keeping the safeguards that matter when spending from a self-custody wallet.

Trust Wallet Debit Card

The debit-style structure fits holders who want spending tied to what they actually own. Each purchase draws against your prepaid balance and connected on-chain collateral—no revolving debt, no interest charges, and no annual fee on any tier. You know what you are spending because it maps to assets you can see in your wallet.

That transparency also makes budgeting easier. You feel the effect of each swipe against your available balance, and rewards return to the same address you linked during setup. For users who treat Trust Wallet as their primary financial hub, a Trust Wallet debit card turns that habit into everyday spending with clear rules and instant digital delivery.
